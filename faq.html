Everything you need to know about BudgetWise

BudgetWise is a free budgeting and financial tracking app that helps you manage your money. You can track expenses, set savings goals, scan receipts, get smart financial advice, and much more. It works on any device with a web browser.

Yes! BudgetWise is a Progressive Web App (PWA) that works on any device. You can install it on your phone's home screen for a native app-like experience. It works on Android, iOS, and desktop browsers.

On your dashboard, tap the "Scan" button next to "Add Expense". Take a photo of your receipt or upload an image, and BudgetWise will automatically extract the amount using OCR (optical character recognition) powered by Tesseract.js. You can then confirm and categorize the expense.

In Family mode, you can add family members (parents, teens, children), set up pocket money/allowances, create chores with rewards, and set shared family savings goals. Family members can be invited to join your family group and track shared spending together.

BudgetWise Junior teaches children to manage money inside your Family account. It covers ages 7 to 17, with lessons grouped into age brackets (7–9, 10–12, 13–15 and 16–17) so the content suits your child. Kids get chores with rewards, a Save / Spend / Give split, short money lessons, and can propose savings goals or ask you for money — which you approve, adjust or decline. A child never creates their own account: you set up their profile and they sign in with a 4-digit PIN, seeing only their own view and never your accounts or expenses.
